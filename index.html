🧪 Chemistry Chapter Notes

1. Chemical Reactions and Equations

  • Exothermic Reaction: Heat is released during the reaction (e.g., Burning of natural gas, Respiration).
  • Endothermic Reaction: Heat is absorbed during the reaction (e.g., Photosynthesis, Decomposition of calcium carbonate).
  • Oxidation: Gain of oxygen or loss of hydrogen.
  • Reduction: Loss of oxygen or gain of hydrogen.

2. Acids, Bases and Salts

  • pH Scale: A scale ranging from 0 (very acidic) to 14 (very alkaline) with 7 being neutral.
  • Plaster of Paris: Chemical formula is CaSO4.1/2H2O. Prepared by heating gypsum at 373 K.
  • Baking Soda: Sodium hydrogen carbonate (NaHCO3), used for making crispy pakoras and as an antacid.

3. Metals and Non-Metals

  • Reactivity Series: Arrangement of metals in decreasing order of their reactivity (Potassium is most reactive, Gold is least reactive).
  • Ionic Bonds: Formed by the complete transfer of electrons from a metal to a non-metal.

⚡ Physics Chapter Notes

1. Light - Reflection and Refraction

  • Laws of Reflection: Angle of incidence is equal to the angle of reflection (i = r).
  • Concave Mirror: Used in torches, search-lights, and vehicles' headlights as reflectors.
  • Convex Mirror: Used as rear-view mirrors in vehicles because they give an erect, diminished image and a wider field of view.

2. Human Eye and the Colorful World

  • Myopia (Near-sightedness): Corrected using a Concave lens.
  • Hypermetropia (Far-sightedness): Corrected using a Convex lens.
  • Dispersion: Splitting of white light into its seven constituent colors through a glass prism.

3. Electricity

  • Ohm's Law: Current flowing through a conductor is directly proportional to the potential difference across its ends at a constant temperature (V = IR).
  • Series Connection: Total resistance is the sum of individual resistances (R = R1 + R2 + R3).
  • Parallel Connection: Reciprocal of total resistance is the sum of reciprocals of individual resistances.

🌱 Biology Chapter Notes

1. Life Processes

  • Autotrophic Nutrition: Mode of nutrition where organisms prepare their own food using sunlight and carbon dioxide (e.g., Green Plants).
  • Heterotrophic Nutrition: Organisms depend on other organisms for food (e.g., Animals, Humans).
  • Nephron: The basic filtering unit of kidneys used for urine formation.

2. Control and Coordination

  • Synapse: The microscopic gap between two adjacent neurons where nerve impulses are transmitted.
  • Reflex Action: A sudden, involuntary response to a stimulus controlled mainly by the spinal cord.
  • Plant Hormones: Auxin (cell elongation), Gibberellin (stem growth), Cytokinin (cell division), and Abscisic acid (wilting of leaves / inhibition).

🎯 Science Practice MCQs & Questions

Q1: Which gas is evolved when dilute hydrochloric acid reacts with reactive metals like zinc?

Ans: Hydrogen gas (H2)

Q2: What is the pH value of a neutral solution at 298 K?

Ans: Exactly 7

Q3: An electric bulb is connected to a 220V source. If the current is 0.50 A, what is the power of the bulb?

Ans: Power (P) = V x I = 220 x 0.50 = 110 Watts

Q4: Which part of the human brain maintains posture and balance of the body?

Ans: Cerebellum

Q5: Name the hormone responsible for the regulation of blood sugar (glucose) level.

Ans: Insulin
