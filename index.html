🎯 Top Practice Questions & MCQs (Board Special)

Q1: Which of the following is a displacement reaction?
(a) $\text{NaCl} + \text{AgNO}_3 \rightarrow \text{AgCl} + \text{NaNO}_3$
(b) $\text{Mg} + \text{2HCl} \rightarrow \text{MgCl}_2 + \text{H}_2$
(c) $\text{NaOH} + \text{HCl} \rightarrow \text{NaCl} + \text{H}_2\text{O}$

Ans: (b) $\text{Mg} + \text{2HCl} \rightarrow \text{MgCl}_2 + \text{H}_2$

Q2: If the distance between two charges is halved, the electric force between them becomes:
(a) Halved    (b) Doubled    (c) Four times    (d) One-fourth

Ans: (c) Four times ($F \propto 1/r^2$)

Q3: Find the roots of the quadratic equation $x^2 - 3x - 10 = 0$.

Ans: $x = 5$ and $x = -2$

Q4: When was the Non-Cooperation Movement called off by Mahatma Gandhi due to the Chauri Chaura incident?

Ans: February 1922
