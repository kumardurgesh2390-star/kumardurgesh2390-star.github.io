================================================== CLASS 10 SCIENCE - COMPREHENSIVE REVISION NOTES ================================================== CHAPTER 1: CHEMICAL REACTIONS AND EQUATIONS - Chemical changes form new substances with new properties (e.g., rusting, cooking, burning). - Balanced equations obey the Law of Conservation of Mass (equal atoms on both sides). - Types of reactions: 1. Combination: A + B -> AB 2. Decomposition: AB -> A + B (Thermal, Electrolytic, Photolytic) 3. Displacement: A + BC -> AC + B (based on reactivity series) 4. Double Displacement: AB + CD -> AD + CB (Precipitation/Neutralization) 5. Redox: Simultaneous oxidation (loss of e-/gain of O) and reduction (gain of e-/loss of O). - Effects: Corrosion (rusting of iron) and Rancidity (oxidation of fats/oils, prevented by antioxidants/nitrogen). CHAPTER 2: ACIDS, BASES AND SALTS - Acids: Sour taste, turn blue litmus red, release H+ ions (hydronium H3O+ in water). - Bases: Bitter/soapy, turn red litmus blue, release OH- ions (alkalis are water-soluble bases). - Dilution safety: Always add acid to water slowly with stirring, never water to acid. - Reactions: Acid + Metal -> Salt + H2; Acid + Metal Carbonate/Bicarbonate -> Salt + H2O + CO2; Acid + Base -> Salt + Water (Neutralization). - pH Scale: 0 to 14 (7 is neutral, <7 acidic, >7 basic). - Important Salts: * NaCl (Common salt) * NaOH (Chlor-alkali process) * Bleaching Powder (CaOCl2) * Baking Soda (NaHCO3) * Washing Soda (Na2CO3.10H2O) * Plaster of Paris (CaSO4.1/2H2O) CHAPTER 4: CARBON AND ITS COMPOUNDS - Carbon is tetravalent and forms strong covalent bonds. Exhibits catenation (self-linking chains). - Allotropes: Diamond (hard, non-conductor), Graphite (soft, good conductor, layered), Fullerenes (spherical C60). - Hydrocarbons: Saturated (Alkanes, CnH2n+2) and Unsaturated (Alkenes CnH2n, Alkynes CnH2n-2). - Chemical Properties: Combustion, Oxidation (using alkaline KMnO4/acidified K2Cr2O7), Addition (hydrogenation of oils), Substitution. - Ethanol (C2H5OH) and Ethanoic Acid (CH3COOH / Acetic acid). - Soaps and Detergents: Soaps form micelles and clean grease, but form scum in hard water. Detergents work in both hard and soft water. CHAPTER 8: HEREDITY AND EVOLUTION - Heredity: Transmission of traits from parents to offspring. - Mendel's Laws: Monohybrid (3:1 ratio) and Dihybrid crosses (9:3:3:1 ratio). Genes exist in pairs (alleles). - Sex Determination: Humans have 23 pairs. Females (XX), Males (XY). Father determines the sex of the child. - Evolution: Gradual changes over generations leading to new species (speciation). Homologous organs (same structure, different function) vs Analogous organs (different structure, same function).
